How we want to do business

The repair matters. So does everything around it.

Good customer service is more than being friendly at the beginning. It means being clear about what we know, what we do not know yet, what you authorized, what changed, and what happens next.

01

Diagnose before guessing

We would rather spend time finding the cause than replace a stack of parts and hope one of them solves the problem. When more testing is needed, we tell you instead of pretending the answer is certain.

02

Approval before added work

If the repair changes, you hear about it first. We explain what was found and what it changes before additional work or cost becomes part of the job.

03

Spend where it makes sense

When different parts or repair paths make sense, we explain the tradeoffs. The goal is not automatically the cheapest part or the most expensive one — it is the choice that makes sense for the vehicle, the repair and your budget.

04

Reputation over one ticket

We want customers who feel comfortable coming back and sending someone they care about. That matters more to us than squeezing the maximum amount out of one repair.

If something does not seem right

Tell us. We want the chance to look at it.

Vehicles are complicated, repairs do not always unfold exactly as expected, and misunderstandings can happen. What matters is what a business does next. If you have a concern about the repair or your experience, we do not want you wondering whether you can bring it up.

Give us the opportunity to hear you out, inspect what needs to be inspected, explain what we find and address the situation fairly to the best of our ability.

1

Tell us what is wrong

Call or text and explain what you are seeing, hearing or feeling. Photos, videos, warning lights and other details can help.

2

We look into it

We review the repair and, when needed, inspect the vehicle so the conversation is based on what is actually happening.

3

We explain what we find

If it relates to our work, if it is a separate problem, or if more diagnosis is needed, we tell you plainly rather than dodge the conversation.

4

We work toward a fair next step

When something is ours to address, we want to address it. When it is not, we still want you to understand why and what your realistic options are.

The right shop for the job

We do not need to keep every job to take care of every customer.

Some repairs are better handled by a shop with a particular specialty, piece of equipment, schedule or setup. If that is the case, we would rather tell you than force a job that is not the right fit.

A good referral is still good service. Our goal is to help you move toward the right solution — whether the repair stays with Shawnee or another reputable local mechanic is better suited for it.

Reviews & honest feedback

We want the reviews to reflect how we really do business.

Good feedback is appreciated. Critical feedback matters too. We do not want public reviews to be a polished version of the business — we want them to be an honest record of the work, the communication and the way concerns are handled.

What that means in practice

We welcome genuine feedback from customers whether it is praise, a suggestion or a concern. If something is bothering you, we would rather hear about it and have the opportunity to address it than have you feel like you were ignored.

Any review you leave is completely your choice. The new-customer offer stands on its own and is never conditioned on leaving a review, giving a particular rating or saying anything specific about Shawnee Auto Repair.
