What Happens Next

From first message to scheduled work.

Shawnee is appointment-based, so the first conversation is used to understand the job before putting it on the schedule.

01

Send the details

Vehicle, location and problem or upgrade goal.

02

Review the job

Fitment, likely parts, repair needs, travel or unusual requirements are checked before scheduling.

03

Approve and schedule

You get the repair plan or audio quote before authorized work begins, then the appointment is scheduled.

No Surprise Add-Ons

If the job changes, the price does not change behind your back.

Added work and added cost require approval before becoming part of the job. Repair parts and Shawnee-supplied audio equipment are billed at actual cost with no parts markup.
