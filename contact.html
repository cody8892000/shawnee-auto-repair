Build a repair request

Give us the useful stuff up front.

This form does not send your information to a database. It simply builds a clear message on your device, then opens your text or email app so you stay in control of what gets sent.

Vehicle & Contact Details

Fields marked with * are needed to build the request.

This form does not schedule an appointment, authorize diagnosis or repair, or create a final estimate. Shawnee will confirm availability and any next steps directly with you. After a text thread opens, you can attach helpful photos, videos or scan-tool screenshots there.

What helps most

Perfect terminology is not required. Your own description of what changed or what the vehicle does is often more useful than guessing at the failed part.

1When the problem started and whether it is constant or intermittent.
2What speed, temperature or situation makes it happen.
3Any lights, codes, noises, smells, leaks or recent repairs.

Photos & videos

After you open a text conversation, send anything useful: scan-tool screens, warning lights, a leak, a broken part, unusual tire wear, or a short video of the noise/problem when safe to capture.

Please do not guess just to fill the form

“It clunks when I turn left” is useful. “I think it needs a CV axle” may or may not be. We would rather start with the symptom and work toward the cause.

If the vehicle may be unsafe

Don’t risk the vehicle — or yourself — just to get it here.

If the vehicle has severe steering or braking problems, major fluid loss, serious overheating, a wheel/bearing concern, smoke, a fuel smell, or another condition that makes driving questionable, tell us before moving it. We can talk through the safest next step.

Scheduled on-site repair is not the same thing as 24/7 emergency roadside assistance. If you are stranded in a dangerous location, prioritize personal safety and appropriate emergency/roadside help first.

Contact questions

Before you send it.

Does sending the repair-request form schedule an appointment?

No. The form creates a message for you to send by text or email. An appointment exists only after Shawnee confirms the date/time and next step with you.

Does sending a message authorize repairs or diagnostic charges?

No. A repair request starts the conversation. Applicable estimates, diagnostic work and repair authorization are handled separately before work proceeds.

Can I send pictures or video?

Yes. The web form itself does not upload files. After opening a text or email conversation, attach useful photos, videos, scan-tool screenshots or warning-light pictures there.

What if I do not know what is wrong?

That is fine. Describe what the vehicle is doing in normal language. You do not need to diagnose the vehicle before contacting us.

What if the vehicle will not run?

Say that in the request and include the vehicle location. We can determine whether on-site work makes sense or whether transport/another arrangement is the better next step.

When can I schedule?

Evenings and weekends are available by appointment. Exact availability depends on the current schedule and the type of job.
