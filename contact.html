Repair request

Use normal language. You do not need to diagnose the vehicle yourself.

Money tight? You can tell us. Assistance is case-by-case and never guaranteed — see how Repair Assistance works.

Sending a request does not schedule an appointment or authorize diagnostic or repair work. Shawnee confirms availability and the next step directly with you.

What helps

Describe the symptom, not your guess.

“It clunks when I turn left” is useful. If you already have a code or prior diagnosis, include that too.

Include the vehicle location.

Location helps determine whether on-site work makes sense or whether the repair should be scheduled at our repair location.

Photos and video can help.

After the text or email opens, attach useful pictures, scan-tool screenshots, warning lights, leaks or a short video of the problem when safe to capture.

If it will not run, say that.

Tell us whether the vehicle runs, drives, is disabled or should not be driven so the next step can be planned correctly.

What happens next

A message starts the conversation.

It does not automatically create an appointment, estimate, diagnostic charge or repair authorization.

1. We review what you sent.

We may ask a few follow-up questions, request a photo/video, or tell you what the next diagnostic step should be.

2. We confirm availability.

Appointment timing depends on the job and current schedule. On-site work is considered when the repair and location make sense.

3. Estimate and authorization come separately.

Applicable estimate, diagnostic and repair authorization are handled before work proceeds. A contact message by itself is not approval to repair the vehicle.

Prefer direct contact?

Call, text or email.

For the fastest start, text the vehicle, location and problem. If you are outside the primary service area, send the location anyway and we can tell you whether the job is workable.
