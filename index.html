About the head unit

The cheapest radio is for features — not maximum volume.

Budget CarPlay units are great for touchscreen, Bluetooth and convenience, but they usually run out of clean volume sooner and are a weaker foundation for amplifiers.

A better head unit does more than add features — it gives the whole system a cleaner, stronger foundation.

Compared with an entry-level budget CarPlay unit, a quality name-brand radio can offer cleaner output, stronger preamp signal, better internal amplification and more usable volume before distortion. Used or refurbished units from brands like Pioneer, Kenwood, JVC, Sony and others can sometimes be found around $135, which means stepping up in quality does not always mean spending hundreds more.

Choose your installation level

Pay for the finish level you actually want.

Every level is safe and functional. You are choosing how much extra labor goes into concealment, factory-style routing, disassembly and fabrication.

Included in starting prices

Standard Installation

The efficient, budget-minded option: safe routing with minimal unnecessary teardown.

Labor level: 1× • Most affordable
  • Safe, fused and secured wiring where required
  • Practical routing with minimal unnecessary disassembly
  • Existing mounting locations used when practical
  • Clean, functional installation without custom fabrication
Upgraded finish

OEM-Style Installation

More hidden wiring, more factory-style routing and a cleaner finished appearance.

Labor level: typically 1.5–3× • Cleaner / more hidden
  • More interior panels, trim or carpet removed as needed
  • Wiring routed with factory harnesses where practical
  • Additional loom, wrapping and concealment
  • More attention to component placement and future serviceability
Highest finish

Custom / Show-Quality

Custom mounting, fabrication, maximum concealment and the highest level of finish.

Labor level: typically 3×+ • Custom / fabrication-heavy
  • Custom amplifier, speaker or equipment mounting
  • Custom panels, adapters or trim work when needed
  • Detailed harness dressing and hidden distribution
  • Project-specific fabrication quoted by the job
Why we offer levels: a $300 audio upgrade should not automatically become a $700 installation just because there is a prettier way to route every wire. We keep the entry level affordable by reducing unnecessary labor — not by eliminating basic electrical safety. If you want the extra teardown, concealment and fabrication, we are happy to quote the additional hours before work begins.

*Starting prices apply to compatible vehicles. Vehicle-specific kits, interfaces, additional wiring, upgraded installation level, fabrication, travel and changing used/refurbished equipment costs can affect the final quote. Final pricing is approved before work begins.

Straight terms

Know what you are paying for before work begins.

The homepage gives you the basics. The full pricing and policy details are on the Pricing page.

Estimate First

Estimates are $25, waived in full when we perform the repair. For repairs covered by Illinois estimate requirements, you receive the required estimate/authorization before work begins. If costs change beyond the authorized amount, we contact you first.

Parts Your Way

Budget, standard and premium options can be discussed when available. Parts are billed at the actual purchase price with no parts markup, and they are itemized on the invoice.

Pay at the End

Nothing is due upfront for ordinary repair work. Payment is due when the authorized work is completed and the itemized invoice is provided.

If a repair cannot be completed as planned:

We stop, explain what was found, and work with you on the next step. You are not charged for repair labor that was not performed. Any diagnostic work, completed authorized work, nonreturnable parts, or third-party towing/transport is handled according to the written estimate and authorization.

When cost is the problem

Before you assume the repair is out of reach, let us quote it.

Because parts are billed at actual cost and different quality levels can be discussed when available, there may be more than one responsible way to approach the same repair. If you are working with a tight budget, say so. We can talk through what needs attention now, what can safely wait, and what options are available. We will not promise every repair can be cheap, and we will not cut safety corners just to hit a number. Ask about Shawnee Repair Assistance — case-by-case help when money is genuinely tight, never guaranteed, and any arrangement is agreed in writing first.
