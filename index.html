Straight terms

Know what you are paying for before work begins.

The homepage gives you the basics. The full pricing and policy details are on the Pricing page.

Estimate First

Estimates are $25, waived in full when we perform the repair. For repairs covered by Illinois estimate requirements, you receive the required estimate/authorization before work begins. If costs change beyond the authorized amount, we contact you first.

Parts Your Way

Budget, standard and premium options can be discussed when available. Parts are billed at the actual purchase price with no parts markup, and they are itemized on the invoice.

Pay at the End

Nothing is due upfront for ordinary repair work. Payment is due when the authorized work is completed and the itemized invoice is provided.

If a repair cannot be completed as planned:

We stop, explain what was found, and work with you on the next step. You are not charged for repair labor that was not performed. Any diagnostic work, completed authorized work, nonreturnable parts, or third-party towing/transport is handled according to the written estimate and authorization.

When cost is the problem

Before you assume the repair is out of reach, let us quote it.

Because parts are billed at actual cost and different quality levels can be discussed when available, there may be more than one responsible way to approach the same repair. If you are working with a tight budget, say so. We can talk through what needs attention now, what can safely wait, and what options are available. We will not promise every repair can be cheap, and we will not cut safety corners just to hit a number. Ask about Shawnee Repair Assistance — case-by-case help when money is the only thing in the way, never guaranteed, and any arrangement is agreed in writing first.
