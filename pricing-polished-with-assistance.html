Repair Pricing

The repair rules are simple.

There is no single published flat labor rate because repair jobs vary in diagnosis, access, time and complexity. Instead, the repair plan and expected cost for your specific job are quoted before any authorized repair work begins. If the job changes, added work and added cost still require your approval first.

$25 Repair Estimate

The estimate fee is waived in full when Shawnee performs the repair.

Parts at Actual Cost

Repair parts Shawnee supplies are billed at the actual purchase price with no parts markup.

Approval Before Added Work

If the job changes, added work and added cost are approved before becoming part of the repair.

If a repair cannot be completed as planned: we stop, explain what was found, and work with you on the next step. Diagnostic work, completed authorized work, nonreturnable parts, or third-party towing/transport are handled according to the written estimate and authorization.

What Can Change a Quote?

Some vehicles simply require more parts or labor.

These are the kinds of things that can legitimately move a mobile-audio quote above the published starting price.

Vehicle-Specific Integration

Dash kits, wiring harnesses, steering-wheel-control interfaces, factory-amplifier integration or CAN/data interfaces.

Extra Wiring or Mounting

Additional wire, unusual mounting requirements, difficult access or extra disassembly beyond a straightforward compatible install.

Installation Finish

Moving from Standard to OEM-Style or Custom / Show-Quality adds labor for routing, concealment and fabrication.

Travel & Equipment Availability

Mobile travel and changing prices/availability on used or refurbished equipment can affect the final quote.

No Parts Markup

Shawnee-supplied parts and audio equipment are billed at actual cost.

Shipping, core credits and similar purchase-price adjustments are passed through instead of being hidden inside a marked-up parts price. Customer-supplied audio equipment is also welcome.

Shawnee Repair Assistance

Need the repair, but money is tight?

Tell us before you assume the repair is out of reach. When someone is genuinely in a bind, we are willing to look at the job and see whether there is a responsible way to make it more manageable. That does not mean every repair can be discounted or financed — it means we are willing to have the conversation instead of treating the first estimate as the only possible path.

Depending on the vehicle, the repair and the circumstances, options may include: prioritizing the repairs that matter most right now; using a less-expensive but responsible parts option when available; splitting non-urgent work into stages when it is mechanically safe; reducing some labor in a genuine hardship situation when we are able; or discussing another reasonable arrangement case by case.

There is no automatic qualification or guaranteed discount. Assistance is entirely case-by-case, and any different payment arrangement or reduced price must be clearly agreed in writing before it applies. Safety still comes first — we never use an unsafe shortcut just because it is cheaper.
