What determines availability

Your address is only part of the answer.

The same town can be an easy yes for one job and a poor fit for another. We look at what the vehicle needs, where it is sitting and what it will take to do the work correctly.

1

Exact location

Send the town or address area so we can judge distance and route realistically.

2

The repair itself

A battery, electrical diagnosis or common repair may make sense on-site. A job needing extended teardown, more equipment or controlled conditions may not.

3

A safe place to work

On-site service needs a reasonably level, accessible and safe work area with permission to perform the repair there.

4

Schedule and route

Nearby jobs may be easier to accommodate when they fit the current route or appointment schedule. We will tell you what is realistic before scheduling.

Where the repair happens

On-site when it makes sense. Repair location when it doesn’t.

The goal is not to force every repair into a driveway. The setting should give the job the best chance of being done safely and correctly.

01

Home or Workplace

On-site service may be available when the location is safe, accessible and the repair can reasonably be completed there.

02

Repair Location

Larger, longer or more involved work can be scheduled at our repair location when the job needs additional space, equipment, time or teardown.

03

Not 24/7 Roadside Service

On-site repair is scheduled service. It is not emergency roadside assistance or towing. If the vehicle cannot be moved safely, tell us before moving it.

Service area questions

Before you send the address.

My town is not listed. Does that mean you do not serve it?

No. The town list shows the general area. Send your location and what the vehicle needs and we can tell you whether it is workable.

Do you come to homes and workplaces?

When the repair and location make sense, yes. The work area needs to be reasonably safe and accessible, and the property owner or responsible party must allow the repair to be performed there.

Do you work at rural addresses?

Yes, rural locations are considered. Accessibility, work area, distance and the type of repair all matter.

Will you come to me for a large repair?

Not always. Larger or more involved repairs may need to be performed at our repair location because of equipment, teardown time, safety or working conditions.

Do you provide emergency roadside assistance?

No. Shawnee Auto Repair offers scheduled on-site repair, not 24/7 emergency roadside service. If the vehicle cannot be moved, contact us with the situation and we can tell you what options make sense.
