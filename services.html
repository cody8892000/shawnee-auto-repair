Common work

Start with the problem.

If your repair is not listed here, ask anyway. This is a practical list of common work, not a limit on every job we will consider.

Diagnostics & Electrical

Check-engine lights, no-starts, stalls, battery drains, wiring faults and electrical problems. Codes are used as a starting point, not treated like a parts list.

  • Check-engine and warning-light diagnosis
  • No-start and intermittent-start concerns
  • Stalling and running problems
  • Parasitic draws, wiring, power and ground faults
  • Sensor and related circuit diagnosis

Starting & Charging

Slow cranking, clicking, dead batteries and charging problems can overlap. We test the system before deciding what actually needs replaced.

  • Battery testing and replacement
  • Starter diagnosis and replacement
  • Alternator and charging-system diagnosis
  • Battery cables, terminals and grounds
  • Intermittent no-crank concerns

Brakes

Brake work should be based on what is worn, damaged or not operating correctly — not automatically replacing everything at once.

  • Pads and rotors
  • Calipers and related hardware
  • Brake hoses and visible leak concerns
  • Grinding, squealing and vibration diagnosis
  • Pulling or uneven braking concerns

Cooling, Belts & Hoses

Overheating and coolant loss can turn expensive quickly. We look for the source of the problem before recommending the repair.

  • Overheating diagnosis
  • Coolant-leak concerns
  • Thermostats and water pumps
  • Belts and tension-related concerns
  • Cooling and service hoses

Steering & Suspension

Noise, looseness, poor handling and uneven movement can come from several components. We inspect what is actually worn before replacing parts.

  • Tie rods and ball joints
  • Control arms
  • Struts and shocks
  • Sway-bar links and related wear items
  • Wheel-bearing and hub concerns when applicable

Audio, Camera & Accessory Installation

Common aftermarket upgrades and installation-related electrical problems when the vehicle and equipment are a good fit.

  • Head units and touchscreen radios
  • CarPlay and Android Auto upgrades
  • Backup-camera installation
  • Speakers and common audio upgrades
  • Bluetooth, microphones and accessory wiring

Pre-Purchase Inspections

A second set of eyes before you buy a used vehicle. The goal is to identify visible problems and concerns before money changes hands.

  • Visible mechanical-condition review
  • Warning lights and scan-tool check when applicable
  • Leaks and obvious wear concerns
  • Steering, suspension and brake observations
  • Test-drive observations when safe and appropriate

A pre-purchase inspection can reduce surprises but cannot guarantee every hidden, intermittent or future defect will be discovered. Pre-purchase inspection pricing is confirmed before scheduling.

General Repair & Maintenance

Not every repair fits neatly into one category. We also take on general mechanical work and routine maintenance when the job fits our equipment and schedule.

  • General mechanical repairs
  • Routine maintenance by request
  • Gauge-cluster concerns
  • Fuel-delivery concerns and related components
  • Belts, hoses and common wear items
  • Light-duty diesel work considered case by case
Diagnosis first

A trouble code is a clue. Not a diagnosis.

A scan tool can tell us where the computer noticed a problem. It does not always tell us which part caused it. Replacing whatever component appears in a code description can waste a lot of money.

When diagnosis is needed, the goal is to test the system, follow the symptoms and find the cause before recommending parts.

Where the work happens

The repair decides the location.

Some jobs make sense on-site. Others need more room, equipment or time. We choose the setting that gives the repair the best chance of being done correctly.

On-site work

Available when the location is safe, accessible and the repair can reasonably be completed there.

Repair location

Larger, longer or more involved repairs can be scheduled at our repair location by appointment.
