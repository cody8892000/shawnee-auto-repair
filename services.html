Repair Services

Common work we handle.

If you do not see your exact problem here, ask. These are service categories, not a limit on every repair we may be able to take on.

Diagnostics & Electrical

Check-engine lights, no-starts, stalls, wiring faults and parasitic draws traced to the cause.

Starting & Charging

Starters, alternators, batteries, cables and charging-system testing and repair.

Brakes

Pads, rotors, calipers, hoses and brake-related concerns inspected and repaired as appropriate.

Cooling & Overheating

Cooling-system and overheating problems, including common jobs such as thermostats and water pumps.

Steering & Suspension

Tie rods, ball joints, control arms, struts, shocks and steering-related problems.

Maintenance & General Repair

Routine maintenance and general mechanical repair for cars, SUVs and light trucks.

Straight Terms

Know what you are paying for before work begins.

The repair estimate, parts policy and authorization process are built around keeping the job understandable before money is spent.

$25 Estimate

Repair estimates are $25 and the fee is waived in full when Shawnee performs the repair. Required estimate and authorization steps are handled before work begins.

Parts at Actual Cost

Budget, standard and premium options can be discussed when available. Parts Shawnee supplies are billed at the actual purchase price with no parts markup.

Approval Before Added Work

If the repair changes, we stop and contact you. Added work and added cost are approved before becoming part of the job.

If a repair cannot be completed as planned: we stop, explain what was found, and work with you on the next step. You are not charged for repair labor that was not performed. Diagnostic work, completed authorized work, nonreturnable parts, or third-party towing/transport are handled according to the written estimate and authorization.
When Cost Is the Problem

Before you assume the repair is out of reach, let us quote it.

Because parts are billed at actual cost and different quality levels can be discussed when available, there may be more than one responsible way to approach the same repair. If the budget is tight, say so. We can talk through what needs attention now, what can safely wait, and what options are available without cutting safety corners just to hit a number.
