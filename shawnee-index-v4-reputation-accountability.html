Straight terms

Know what you’re paying for before the wrench turns.

Shawnee Auto Repair is built around straightforward estimates, real parts pricing and clear authorization. No mystery line items and no extra repair added because “we were already in there.”

Estimate First

Estimates are $25, waived in full when we perform the repair. For repairs covered by Illinois estimate requirements, you receive the required estimate/authorization before work begins. If costs change beyond the authorized amount, we contact you first.

Parts Your Way

Budget, standard and premium options can be discussed when available. Parts are billed at the actual purchase price with no parts markup, and they are itemized on the invoice.

Pay at the End

Nothing is due upfront for ordinary repair work. Payment is due when the authorized work is completed and the itemized invoice is provided.

If a repair cannot be completed as planned:

We stop, explain what was found, and work with you on the next step. You are not charged for repair labor that was not performed. Any diagnostic work, completed authorized work, nonreturnable parts, or third-party towing/transport is handled according to the written estimate and authorization.

Frequently asked questions

The stuff you want to know before you schedule.

Do you come to me, or do I bring the vehicle to you?

Both. Work that can reasonably be done at your home or workplace may be handled on-site. Larger repairs are handled at our repair location by appointment. If transport is needed, the plan is discussed with you before anything moves.

What does an estimate cost?

$25. If Shawnee Auto Repair performs the repair, the estimate fee is waived in full. You pay the estimate fee when you take the diagnosis/estimate and have the work performed elsewhere.

Do I pay anything upfront?

For ordinary repair work, no. Payment is due when the authorized work is completed and you receive the itemized invoice.

How are parts priced?

Parts are billed at the actual purchase price with no parts markup. When multiple quality levels are available, we can discuss budget, standard and premium options before ordering.

What if you find something else wrong during the repair?

We contact you first. Additional work is not simply added to the bill. You approve the changed repair plan before we proceed beyond the amount you already authorized.

What if the repair cannot be completed as originally planned?

We stop and explain why. You are not charged for repair labor that was not performed. Diagnostic work, completed authorized work, nonreturnable parts, or third-party transport may still apply when they were disclosed and authorized.

When are you available?

Evenings and weekends are available by appointment. Call, text, or email to get on the schedule.

Do you work on diesels?

Ask about light-duty diesel work. Availability depends on the specific job, and work requiring specialized diesel equipment may be referred out when that is the better option.
