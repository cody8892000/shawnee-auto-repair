Estimates

Start with the repair plan and the expected cost.

The estimate is there so you can decide what you want done before the repair starts—not after the bill is already written.

$25 estimate

Estimates are $25, waived in full when we perform the repair. For repairs covered by Illinois estimate requirements, you receive the required estimate/authorization before work begins. If costs change beyond the authorized amount, we contact you first.

When diagnosis takes more

Some electrical, intermittent, drivability or hard-to-reproduce problems need additional testing before anyone can responsibly say what failed. If additional diagnostic work is needed, we explain what is recommended and what it will cost before that work proceeds.

Parts Pricing

Parts at actual cost. No parts markup.

The part itself should not become another hidden profit center. When Shawnee purchases a part for your repair, you are billed what it actually costs us to obtain it.

What that means in practice

  • Budget, standard and premium options can be discussed when useful choices exist.
  • Major parts are identified on the estimate or invoice so you can see what was used.
What “actual cost” means:

The amount Shawnee actually pays to obtain the part for your repair, with no extra percentage or house markup added by Shawnee simply for reselling the part.

Special-order or nonreturnable parts

If a job requires a part that cannot reasonably be returned or canceled, we tell you before it is ordered. Any exception to the ordinary no-upfront-payment policy must be discussed and approved first.

Customer-supplied parts

Ask first. Customer-supplied parts may be considered case by case depending on fitment, quality, safety and the job. Warranty responsibility can differ when the part was purchased elsewhere.

Part warranties

Manufacturer and supplier warranty terms vary. We pass along the warranty information that applies to the part used. Any Shawnee labor or workmanship coverage offered for a specific repair will be stated with that job rather than implied as a blanket promise.

Before you assume the repair is out of reach, let us quote it.

Actual-cost parts and different responsible part options can make a real difference in the total. When it is mechanically safe to do so, we can also talk through what needs attention now and what can wait. We cannot promise every repair will be inexpensive, and we will not cut safety corners to hit a number—but if cost is the reason you are putting off the repair, tell us.

Authorization

If the job changes, the conversation happens first.

Illinois law gives consumers specific estimate and authorization rights. Shawnee’s day-to-day policy is straightforward: added work and added cost are discussed before they become part of the job.

1

We explain the proposed repair

You receive the repair plan and expected charges required for the work being proposed.

2

You authorize the work

Approval is documented in the manner appropriate for the job before the authorized repair moves forward.

3

Something changes? We stop and contact you.

Unexpected damage, additional failed components, extra diagnostic needs or other changes are discussed before they become additional authorized work.

4

You receive an itemized invoice

Completed work, parts and applicable authorized charges are shown when the job is finished.

Illinois consumer protections still apply.

Shawnee’s policies are intended to work alongside Illinois automotive-repair protections, not replace or reduce them.

Payment & Exceptions

Nothing due upfront for ordinary repair work.

That policy applies to ordinary repair work. Unusual parts, outside services or other special circumstances are discussed before any different arrangement applies.

Ordinary repairs

Nothing is due upfront for ordinary repair work. Payment is due when the authorized work is completed and the itemized invoice is provided.

Special orders

If a customer-approved job requires a specifically ordered, nonreturnable item or outside service that requires advance payment, any exception is explained before the order or commitment is made.

Towing / transport / outside services

Third-party towing, transport, machine work or other outside services are not silently folded into a repair. If they are needed, the expected charge or arrangement is discussed before authorization whenever practicable.

If the repair cannot be finished

You are not charged repair labor that was not performed. Authorized diagnostic work, completed authorized work, installed parts, specifically ordered nonreturnable parts or approved third-party charges may still apply.

New Customer Offer

Labor is quoted in writing before work begins. New customers get 25% off labor on their first completed repair. One first-job offer per new customer. Labor only; parts and third-party charges are excluded.

Pricing Questions

The details people usually ask about.

If something about your specific estimate is unclear, ask before authorizing it. The written estimate for your vehicle controls the actual job.

Is the $25 estimate always the only diagnostic charge?

No. The standard estimate is $25 and is waived if Shawnee performs the repair. Some complex or intermittent problems require additional diagnostic time or testing. If that is needed, the additional diagnostic work and cost are discussed and authorized before proceeding.

What happens if I get the estimate and decide not to repair the vehicle?

If no additional diagnostic work or other authorized charges were involved, the standard $25 estimate fee is due. If you authorized deeper diagnostic work, nonreturnable special-order parts or a third-party service, those authorized charges may also apply.

Does “parts at actual cost” mean the cheapest part available?

No. It describes how the part is priced, not which quality level you must choose. When useful options are available, we can discuss budget, standard and premium choices and the tradeoffs between them.

What if you discover another problem after work starts?

We contact you before additional work becomes part of the job. We explain what was found, what we recommend and how it changes the expected cost so you can decide whether to proceed.

Can I bring my own parts?

Possibly. Customer-supplied parts are considered case by case. Fitment, part quality, safety and warranty responsibility all matter, so ask before buying or bringing the part.

Questions Before You Authorize?

Ask before the wrench turns.

If anything about the estimate, part choice, labor, repair options or payment terms is unclear, ask. We would rather explain it before the work than argue about it after the invoice.
