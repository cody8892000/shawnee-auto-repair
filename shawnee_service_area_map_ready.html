Repair vs Mobile Audio

The travel side works a little differently.

Both sides are appointment-based, but the location requirements are not identical.

Auto Repair

Mechanical repair is scheduled around the job.

Repair location depends on what the vehicle needs, how involved the work is and whether the job is appropriate for the available repair setup.

  • Send the vehicle location with the repair request
  • More involved repairs are handled at the repair location by appointment
  • Some jobs may be referred if another facility or specialty is the better fit
Mobile Audio

Audio installation can come to you when the site works.

A mobile install needs a reasonably safe, level work area with enough room to access the vehicle. Weather, lighting and job complexity can affect whether the location makes sense.

  • Driveways and other suitable private work areas can work
  • Travel can affect the final quote
  • Large or fabrication-heavy jobs may need a different setup
Location Rule Send your location with the vehicle information. If travel, access or the work site changes the job, that is discussed before you schedule.
