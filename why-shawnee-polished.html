The Shawnee Approach

Good service should make the decision easier, not harder.

The point is not to sell the biggest repair or the most expensive audio equipment. It is to understand what you want, show you the realistic options, and let you decide what makes sense.

01

Find the real problem

On repair work, start with the complaint and work toward the cause. On audio, start with the goal and build the system around what the customer actually wants.

02

Explain the options

What was found, what matters now, what can wait, and what different equipment or repair choices change.

03

Quote before work

You should know the plan and the expected cost before authorized work begins.

04

Ask before adding

If something changes during the job, added work and added cost are approved before moving forward.

05

Keep the budget in the conversation

If money is tight, say so. When responsible alternatives exist, we can talk through them.

06

Know when to refer

Some work needs specialty equipment, another facility or a different shop. When that is the better answer, we’ll tell you.

Budget Matters

Tell us what you can realistically spend.

Honest Referrals

Not every job belongs here.

If a repair needs dealership-level programming, specialty equipment, a lift, machine work or a facility setup we do not have, we will tell you before the job turns into wasted time or unnecessary cost. The same applies to mobile audio work that needs fabrication or conditions that do not make sense for a mobile install.

That may mean recommending a dealer, specialty shop, machine shop, alignment shop, fabrication shop or another installer when that is the better path. The point is to help you get the job handled correctly—not keep every job under the Shawnee name.

“If another shop is the better fit, we’ll say so.”
In practice, that means stopping before money gets wasted and pointing you toward the equipment, facility or specialty the job actually needs.
